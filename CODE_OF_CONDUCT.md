# Code of Conduct

The `ggcircular` project aims to be a respectful and useful space for people
working with circular statistics, data visualization, R programming and
reproducible research.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good faith while remaining precise about technical issues.
- Discuss statistical limitations clearly and objectively.
- Keep feedback focused on the code, documentation, data or analysis.
- Welcome questions from users with different levels of R or statistics
  experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.
- Dismissing reproducibility, validity or safety concerns without engagement.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the package maintainer at the email address
listed in `DESCRIPTION`. Reports will be reviewed as promptly as possible.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues or block participation
when behavior makes the project less safe or less productive for contributors
and users.
